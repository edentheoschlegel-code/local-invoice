Common questions

Will my invoices sync to my other devices?
No — by design. Local Invoice stores everything only on the device you're using, with no account and no cloud sync. If you need an invoice on another device, export it as a PDF and share it over (AirDrop, email, etc.).

I deleted the app / cleared my browser data — can I get my invoices back?
Only from a backup you made yourself. Since nothing is ever uploaded, there's no copy on our end to restore from. If you saved a backup file (footer → Back up your data), you can import it with Restore from backup. This is the tradeoff for full privacy — see the Privacy Policy for details.

Is there a paid plan?
Local Invoice is free to use, with an optional Pro unlock: a one-time $12.99 purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks custom logo & branding, payment instructions, and recording partial payments & deposits on every invoice.

Checkout is handled securely by Stripe (via RevenueCat) — the processor only ever sees the email for your receipt and your payment method, never your invoices or client data. Pro comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee: if it's not for you, email support@localinvoiceapp.com within 30 days for a full refund. Once approved, your refund takes about 5–10 business days to appear on your statement.

On iPhone and iPad, checkout is handled by Apple through the App Store — we never see your card, name, or email. If Pro isn't for you, you can request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple reviews and processes it.

How do I get Pro back on a new device?
When you unlock Pro, you're given a one-time restore code (and a license card you can save). Since there are no accounts, that code is how you re-unlock Pro in your web browser on any device — keep it somewhere safe.

Hold onto your Stripe receipt email as proof of purchase, too. Lost your code? Email support@localinvoiceapp.com and we'll help. The iPhone and iPad app sells Pro separately through the App Store.

How do I get Pro back on a new device?
Tap Restore Purchases and sign in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with — Apple brings your purchase back. Your Apple receipt is your proof of purchase. Bought Pro on the web instead? Web and App Store purchases are separate — your restore code works in your browser.

Does Local Invoice work offline?
Yes, once loaded. There's no server it depends on for day-to-day use.
